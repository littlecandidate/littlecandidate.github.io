Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Little Candidate Education Limited ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Little Candidate Education Limited.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve our educational services. This may include basic account information and voice data necessary for our AI to provide pronunciation and logic feedback. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for:

  • Providing our AI mock interview services.
  • Improving our natural language processing (NLP) models.
  • Generating aggregated, anonymous peer benchmarking data.

3. Children's Privacy and Voice Data

Our service is designed for early childhood education (K1-K3), but accounts must be created and managed by parents or legal guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parental consent.

Any voice data collected during AI mock interviews is processed solely for providing immediate pronunciation and logic feedback. We do not use voice data to identify the child, and audio recordings are handled securely. Parents can review or request deletion of this data at any time.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request data deletion, please contact us via the email provided below. We will process your request securely and promptly in accordance with applicable laws.
